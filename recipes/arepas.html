Arepas

Description

An arepa is a round, flat cornmeal patty that is grilled, fried, or baked until golden and slightly crispy on the outside, yet soft and steamy within. In Venezuela, it is sliced open and stuffed with countless fillings, from shredded beef and black beans to cheese and avocado, serving as the beloved everyday bread and comfort food of the country.

Ingredients

Making an Arepa is really easy, you only need 3 ingredients:

  1. Water
  2. Precooked Corn Flour, can be found as "masarepa" or Yellow Corn Meal
  3. Salt

Steps

  • Pour water and corn flour in a bowl, add salt as desired and mix away until you have a consistent dough.
  • Then, separate the dough in small balls, and give them shape! Usually, this means doing a motion similar to using clay, where you smash it softly as it gets shape.
  • After you have them shaped, put them into a heated pan, oven, air fryer, grill, oil fryer, you name it!
  • Finally, after they are cooked, just open them through the middle like a sandwich, and fill them with whatever ingredient you want, and enjoy your arepas!
