Omelette

Description

AA classic omelette begins as a simple mixture of beaten eggs, often seasoned with salt and pepper, poured into a hot buttered pan. As the eggs set, the cook gently folds the edges toward the center, creating soft, tender curds while the bottom turns a pale golden color. The result is a delicate, pillowy envelope that holds its shape yet remains moist and fluffy inside.

What makes an omelette truly versatile is its filling; anything from melted cheese, sautéed mushrooms, and spinach to diced ham, onions, and fresh herbs can be tucked inside before the final fold. Whether served plain and minimalist or generously stuffed, the omelette is a quick, elegant dish equally at home on a breakfast table or a leisurely brunch plate.

Ingredients

  1. Any amount of eggs you want.
  2. Any amount of filling you want, we'll use 2 here.
  3. We will use ham and cheese.
  4. Salt and pepper for the seasoning.

Steps

  • In a small bowl, pour your whole eggs, add salt to your liking, and whisk until the mix becomes uniform in color - remember to taste!
  • Turn on the heat of your frying pan, apply a coat of oil, and pour the eggs onto the pan. Spread properly to ensure uniformity of the omellete later on.
  • When the mixture starts to get fully cooked on the edges, add your filling in the middle of the omelette, and gently, with the help of a fork or a spatula, press either half of the egg mixture on top of the other half, OR if you prefer it, put one half first and then the other half; either way, press the egg on top of the filling, and let the egg cook itself a little longer.
  • Finally, take it off the pan and serve it on the plate, add freshly grinded pepper on top for extra spicy taste, and voila! Bon appetit.
