Root Beer Float

Description

A root beer float is a classic American dessert made by pouring cold, fizzy root beer over a scoop of creamy vanilla ice cream in a tall glass. The result is a frothy, foamy top with a sweet, vanilla-spiked soda below, best enjoyed immediately with a straw and a spoon.

Ingredients

  1. Root Beer
  2. Vanilla Ice Cream
  3. Any topping you preffer like whipped cream, cherries or cinnamon

Steps

  • Before doing anything, place a tall glass in the freezer for 15 minutes.
  • Then, add 2 scoops or whatever amount you want into the chilled glass that we mentioned before.
  • Now, pour the root beer veeery slowly, while tilting the glass slightly to reduce the foaming, but not fully, just about 3/4 of the glass to let the foam settle. Only then - add more root beer.
  • Finally, add your preferred toppings, a straw, a spoon, and enjoy!
